How to read this page
  • Before a device can reach a site it asks for the site's address. That question is a lookup. netinv counts lookups, so it knows which sites a device contacted and roughly how often. It cannot see pages, messages, or how much data moved.
  • A device name marked guess was worked out from what the device looks up. Give the device a name to replace the guess.
  • Devices using a VPN, iCloud Private Relay or their own private DNS show little or nothing here. netinv flags them when it can tell.
  • Blocking a site makes its lookups fail for the whole network. It can take a few minutes to kick in, and it cannot block a single device.
